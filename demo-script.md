# Demo video script — StokLaris AI (2–4 minutes)
# Record on a phone: screen-record the app + voiceover in English.
# (UI is Indonesian; narrate in English and point at what each part means.)

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem
[Show: a warung shelf / paper notebook, then the phone]
"Indonesia has more than 64 million micro-businesses. Most warung owners still
track stock on paper — or in their heads. They find out the coffee is gone only
when a customer asks for it. Existing POS apps cost up to ninety-nine thousand
rupiah a month, and they make you tap through forms for every single sale.
StokLaris does it differently."

## 0:20–1:00 — Type one sentence, AI understands
[Show: input box, type "jual kopi 5 @3000"]
"Watch this. I type one sentence, the way an owner would say it: jual kopi 5
at 3000. Before I save anything, the AI shows me what it understood: a sale,
kopi, five pieces, three thousand rupiah each, category: drinks. If it gets
something wrong, I see it here — it never saves a silent mistake."
[Tap Simpan. Show the history row appear.]
"One more: beli gula 2kg Rp26.000 — a purchase, two kilos, twenty-six thousand
total. Same thing: understood, confirmed, saved."

## 1:00–1:50 — Dashboard, alerts, forecast
[Scroll to "Hari ini" and "Peringatan stok"]
"Today's revenue and profit are live — profit is revenue minus the average
purchase cost, and if an item was never bought through the app, it says so
instead of inventing a number."
[Point at the alerts]
"Here is the part owners actually need: the forecast. It looks at the last
fourteen days of sales and tells me the coffee runs out in about three-point-
seven days, and suggests ordering thirty-three pieces to cover a week. Same for
the noodles and the water."

## 1:50–2:40 — The stock table + privacy
[Scroll to "Stok & prakiraan"]
"Every item, its remaining stock, how fast it sells, when it runs out, and its
status. And all of this — the parsing, the categorization, the forecast — runs
one hundred percent on the device. No account, no server, no API key, no data
leaving the phone. A warung owner's sales data is their livelihood. It should
not have to be uploaded anywhere to get a stock alert."

## 2:40–3:00 — Close
[Show the phone home screen / app once more]
"StokLaris AI. Type one sentence. Know your stock, your profit, and what to
buy next. Free, offline, in Bahasa Indonesia. Thank you."
